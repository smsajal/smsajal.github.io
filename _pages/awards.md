## Research Awards

**Best Paper Runner-Up Award** — EuroSys 2024 _(April 2024)_
: Awarded for [TraceUpscaler: Upscaling Traces to Evaluate Systems at High Load](/assets/pdf/eurosys24-traceupscaler.pdf) at the 19th ACM European Conference on Computer Systems. EuroSys is a premier venue in computer systems research with a highly competitive acceptance process (~17% acceptance rate).

**Production Deployment** — Azure, Microsoft _(2023)_
: [Kerveros](/assets/pdf/osdi23-sajal.pdf), published at OSDI 2023, was deployed in Azure production — a rare distinction for an academic research paper. This deployment serves as direct evidence of national and international significance, as Azure is one of the world's largest cloud platforms.
